flowDraw

Draw in the air using only your hand. No mouse, no touchscreen — just your index finger and a webcam. Built with real-time hand tracking via MediaPipe, flowDraw maps your fingertip position to a live canvas so you can sketch, doodle, and create without ever touching a device.

⭐ Featured // eye-tracking · game · webgazer

RETINA

A space shooter you aim with your eyes. No mouse, no keyboard — just where you look. Uses WebGazer.js to track your gaze in real time, calibrating to your screen so your ship fires wherever your eyes are pointed. Waves get harder. Don't let them through.
